Text message notifications on Chavrusa Link

Chavrusa Link helps people find a chavrusa to learn with. Members may choose to be notified by text message instead of, or in addition to, email.

How members opt in

On their own profile page, after signing in, a member enters their mobile number and verifies it with a one-time code sent by SMS. Only a number they have verified can be used. A section headed “How we reach you” then offers three choices: Email, Text message, and Email and text message. Email is the default; the two text options stay disabled until the number is verified, and choosing one of them is the opt-in. No member is opted in automatically.

What we send

Only notifications about the member’s own activity: someone asked to learn with them, their request was accepted, a time was suggested or confirmed, or another member sent them a message. No marketing or promotional messages.

How often

Only when one of those things happens — typically a few messages a month, and never more than twelve a day.

Message and data rates may apply.

To stop

Reply STOP to any message. Texts stop immediately and notifications go back to email. Reply HELP for help, or write to info@chavrusalink.com.
